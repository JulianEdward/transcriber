# Julian's Transcriber

Drop in a voice note in Indonesian, English or both. It separates the speakers, tags each line's language and gives you everything in natural English. Export the transcript as text, subtitles or PDF.

**Open it:** https://julianedward.github.io/transcriber/

It runs in your browser and uses Google's Gemini with your own free API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey). The key and your transcripts stay in your browser; audio goes straight from your browser to Gemini.
