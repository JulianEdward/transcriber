Connect Gemini for free

This Transcriber uses Google's Gemini to listen to your voice notes. It needs your own free API key, which takes about a minute to get.

  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Choose Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below.

The key is saved in this browser only. The free tier gives you a set number of requests per day; when the main model runs out, This Transcriber switches to Gemini's lighter model. On the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products, so keep confidential recordings for a key with billing turned on.
